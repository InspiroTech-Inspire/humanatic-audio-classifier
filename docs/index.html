Audio-to-Text Classifier

A Chrome extension that detects audio on a page, transcribes it with Whisper, and classifies the call with OpenAI.

Download latest release

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open the extension's Settings and add your Whisper URL and OpenAI key.
